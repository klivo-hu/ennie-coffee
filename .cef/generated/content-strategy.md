# Content Strategy

Tone of voice: Warm, plain, and confident — human and specific.

## Content hierarchy

- Dashboard
- Home
- Reservations
- Sign Up
- About
- Admin
- Contact
- Login
- Menu

## Page objectives

- **dashboard** — The authenticated application home. (CTA: Learn more)
- **home** — Communicate the core value and route visitors to conversion. (CTA: Start — reservation)
- **reservations** — Capture bookings. (CTA: Reserve)
- **signup** — Register new users. (CTA: Create account)
- **about** — Build trust through story, team, and credibility. (CTA: Learn more)
- **admin** — Back-office management. (CTA: Learn more)
- **contact** — Capture inquiries and provide details. (CTA: Send message)
- **login** — Authenticate returning users. (CTA: Learn more)
- **menu** — Present the offering. (CTA: Learn more)
- **blog** — Publish content for SEO and authority. (CTA: Learn more)
- **gallery** — Show the space or work visually. (CTA: Learn more)
- **legal** — Required legal and policy pages. (CTA: Learn more)

## CTA strategy

One primary CTA per page, all leading toward "reservation".

## Trust-building

- reviews
- ratings
- photos
- awards

## Headline guidance

- Lead with the benefit, not the feature.
- Be specific and concrete; avoid generic superlatives.
- Keep the primary headline to one clear idea.

## Internal linking

- Navigation exposes the top-priority pages.
- Contextual links connect related content.
- Every page offers a next step toward conversion.

# Design craft floor

**Skill:** `impeccable` · https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable · Apache-2.0
**Surface mode:** operate — The visitor completes a task.

Every interface in this project is designed through the Impeccable skill and judged against
the catalog below. The checks run on the built result, not on the intention.

## Mode: operate

Scanability, consistency, and native expectations outrank expression. Brand lives in precise details, not in decoration.

## Verify

- **Contrast** (CF-01) — Body and placeholder text ≥ 4.5:1, large text ≥ 3:1, verified in both themes. On colored surfaces, tint secondary text from that hue — never neutral gray.
- **Depth** (CF-02) — Shadows carry a real offset and a soft blur. A zero-offset colored halo is decoration, not elevation.
- **Spacing** (CF-03) — Tight groups, generous separation, and more space above a heading than below it. Read the computed values rather than trusting the classes.
- **Type** (CF-04) — Body measure 65–75ch, display capped at 6rem, tracking floor −0.04em, balanced headings, and obvious size and weight steps. Run the real copy at every breakpoint.
- **Motion** (CF-05) — One authored moment, not scattered effects and not one identical entrance per section. Exponential ease-out from an already-visible default, honoring reduced motion.
- **States** (CF-06) — Hover, focus, disabled, loading, error, and empty — with real content, working controls, and a responsive composition.
- **Browser surfaces** (CF-07) — Theme the parts you did not draw: text selection, caret, scrollbars, focus rings, underline offset, and tabular numerals. The cheapest signal that a page was built rather than assembled.
- **Copy** (CF-08) — The product's own language. Controls name their action; errors name the problem and the recovery.
- **Coverage** (CF-09) — Every brief requirement present and findable within seconds.

## Refuse

These are the category defaults. Reaching for one when the axis is free means no decision was
made; the element is rewritten, not softened.

- Same-size cards of icon + heading + text as the page structure. Cards are the lazy container; nested cards are always wrong.
- The hero-metric template: big number, small label, supporting stats, accent.
- A kicker or eyebrow above a heading. This one is a ban, not a default — no brief earns it back.
- Section numbers (01 / 02 / 03) unless the sequence itself carries information the reader needs.
- A modal for a task that needs neither interruption nor protected focus.
- Gradient text. Emphasis comes from weight or size.
- Glass and blur as decoration rather than as a specific effect.
- A colored left or right border above 1px on cards, list items, callouts, or alerts.
- Hard offset shadows outside a world that is actually neobrutalist.
- Sparklines, progress rings, and soft-shadowed rounded rectangles standing in for content.
- Monospace as a costume for "technical" rather than for code, data, or measurement.
- A system display face as the display voice of an own-world page.
- Unicode glyphs or emoji standing in for an icon system.
- Geometric masks standing in for organic contours.
- Light or dark picked by category rather than by the use scene.

## Catalog

59 rules. `blocker` never ships, `major` clears before completion,
`advisory` may be waived with a reason recorded in `.claude/memory/decisions.md`.

| Rule                                                     | Severity | Refuses                                                                                                                                                                                        | Instead                                                                                                                                   |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | -------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| IM-001 Side-tab accent border                            | major    | A thick colored border on one side of a card, list item, callout, or alert — the single most recognizable tell of a generated interface.                                                       | Carry the accent in the content: a stronger heading, a token background, or a 1px border on all sides.                                    |
| IM-002 Border accent on a rounded element                | major    | A thick accent border on a rounded card, where the straight stripe fights the corner radius.                                                                                                   | Commit to one — the radius or the edge. Drop the accent border, keep the shape.                                                           |
| IM-054 Hairline border with wide shadow                  | major    | A hairline border paired with a wide, diffuse shadow — a defined edge and a soft elevation claimed at once.                                                                                    | Choose one depth language: a defined edge, or an elevation token. Never both.                                                             |
| IM-055 Repeating-gradient stripes                        | major    | Repeating-gradient stripes used as surface decoration.                                                                                                                                         | Leave the surface plain, or use a deliberate texture that the brand actually owns.                                                        |
| IM-056 Decorative grid-line background                   | major    | A grid or line field drawn with hairline gradients tiled to a fixed pixel cell, used as page decoration.                                                                                       | Reserve grid overlays for canvas, map, blueprint, or measurement surfaces. Elsewhere use product structure or a plain surface.            |
| IM-052 Radius outside the design system                  | advisory | A border-radius literal that is not on the documented radius scale.                                                                                                                            | Use a radius token, or add the new step to the design system deliberately and record why.                                                 |
| IM-003 Overused font                                     | major    | Inter, Roboto, Fraunces, Geist, Plus Jakarta Sans, and Space Grotesk. Every wave of generated interfaces converges on the same handful of faces, so they no longer read as a choice.           | Choose a face that gives the interface a voice, and record the choice in the design system.                                               |
| IM-004 Flat type hierarchy                               | major    | Type sizes packed so closely together that no step reads as a level.                                                                                                                           | Use fewer steps with more contrast — at least a 1.25 ratio between adjacent sizes.                                                        |
| IM-018 Icon tile stacked above a heading                 | major    | A small rounded-square icon container sitting above a heading — the universal generated feature card.                                                                                          | Set the icon beside the heading, or let it sit in flow without a container of its own.                                                    |
| IM-019 Italic serif display headline                     | advisory | Oversized italic serif as the primary hero headline — taste in isolation, the default AI startup hero in aggregate.                                                                            | Set the headline roman, or move to a non-serif display face. An editorial register may earn the italic; judge it against the brief.       |
| IM-020 Hero eyebrow or pill chip                         | major    | A tiny uppercase tracked label — or the same shape as a pill chip — immediately above an oversized hero headline.                                                                              | Work the words into the headline, or drop them. The headline carries its own weight.                                                      |
| IM-021 Kicker above a heading                            | major    | A tracked uppercase or small-caps label standing as its own block directly above a heading. Banned outright — no brief earns it back.                                                          | Delete the label. If the words matter, they belong in the heading or the body.                                                            |
| IM-022 Numbered section labels                           | major    | Small numeric index labels (01 / 02 / 03) riding beside section headings — a page numbering its own chapters instead of earning structure.                                                     | Let hierarchy, content, and rhythm carry the sequence, unless the order itself is information the reader needs.                           |
| IM-026 Oversized hero headline                           | advisory | A full-sentence headline set at display size, so it fills the viewport and leaves nothing above the fold.                                                                                      | Tighten the copy to one or two words at that size, or set the long headline smaller.                                                      |
| IM-027 Crushed letter spacing                            | major    | Letter spacing pulled past −0.04em, where characters stop keeping their own shapes and legibility pays for the look.                                                                           | Tighten display type optically, never destructively. −0.04em is the floor.                                                                |
| IM-039 Tight line height                                 | major    | Line height below 1.3 on multi-line text.                                                                                                                                                      | Set body leading between 1.5 and 1.7 so lines have room to breathe.                                                                       |
| IM-040 Skipped heading level                             | blocker  | A heading level jumped (h1 → h3). Screen readers navigate by outline; a skipped level breaks the document.                                                                                     | Descend one level at a time. Style with tokens, never with the heading tag.                                                               |
| IM-042 Justified text                                    | major    | Justified body text without hyphenation, which opens rivers of white between words.                                                                                                            | Left-align body text, or enable `hyphens: auto` if justification is truly required.                                                       |
| IM-043 Tiny body text                                    | blocker  | Body text below 12px, which fails on high-DPI screens and small viewports.                                                                                                                     | Set body text to at least 14px; 16px is the working default.                                                                              |
| IM-044 Undersized functional text                        | blocker  | Interactive or content-bearing UI text below 11px — links, buttons, nav items, labels, table cells, meta rows. Being on the type ramp does not exempt the value; that only launders the token. | Hold an 11px floor for functional text (10px for non-interactive legal smallprint). `sr-only`, `sup`/`sub`, and code contexts are exempt. |
| IM-045 All-caps body text                                | major    | Long passages in uppercase. Words are recognized by shape, and uppercase removes the ascenders and descenders that carry it.                                                                   | Reserve uppercase for short labels and headings; set passages in sentence case.                                                           |
| IM-046 Wide letter spacing on body text                  | major    | Letter spacing above 0.05em on body text, which breaks natural character groupings and slows reading.                                                                                          | Reserve wide tracking for short uppercase labels.                                                                                         |
| IM-050 Font outside the design system                    | advisory | A font family that the design system does not declare.                                                                                                                                         | Use the documented type system, or add the face deliberately and record why.                                                              |
| IM-053 Font size outside the type ramp                   | advisory | A font-size literal that is off the documented type ramp.                                                                                                                                      | Use a documented step, or extend the ramp deliberately and record why.                                                                    |
| IM-005 Gradient text                                     | major    | Gradient fills on text — decorative rather than meaningful, and most recognizable on headings and metrics.                                                                                     | Set text in a solid token color. Emphasis comes from weight and size.                                                                     |
| IM-006 Generated color palette                           | major    | Purple-to-blue gradients and cyan-on-dark — the two most recognizable palettes of generated interfaces.                                                                                        | Derive the palette from the brand, and record it in the design system.                                                                    |
| IM-007 Cream or beige default surface                    | advisory | A warm cream or beige page background reached for by reflex as the "tasteful" default surface.                                                                                                 | Take the background from a deliberate palette, chosen for this product.                                                                   |
| IM-014 Glowing shadow accents                            | major    | Zero-offset chromatic halos, and any colored blurred shadow on a dark background — the default "cool" look of generated interfaces.                                                            | Use neutral elevation tokens with a real offset and a soft blur.                                                                          |
| IM-015 Radial-gradient background halo                   | major    | A saturated radial wash fading to transparent behind a dark page — the glow tell drawn as a gradient.                                                                                          | Ground the surface with a solid or subtly shifted background token.                                                                       |
| IM-016 Decorative radial spotlight                       | major    | A low-opacity accent radial gradient dropped behind a hero as a "spotlight" — the translucent cousin of the halo.                                                                              | Let the surface stand on its own, or light the composition with a deliberate material accent.                                             |
| IM-034 Gray text on a colored background                 | blocker  | Neutral gray text sitting on a colored surface, where it reads as washed out.                                                                                                                  | Tint secondary text from the surface hue, or use the foreground token. Never neutral gray on color.                                       |
| IM-035 Low contrast text                                 | blocker  | Text below WCAG 2.2 AA — 4.5:1 for body and placeholders, 3:1 for large text. This is a floor, not a preference.                                                                               | Raise the contrast until the pair passes AA in both themes.                                                                               |
| IM-051 Color outside the palette                         | advisory | A literal color outside the documented palette and its tonal ramps.                                                                                                                            | Use a color token, or add the value to the palette deliberately and record the decision.                                                  |
| IM-008 Nested cards                                      | major    | A card inside a card. Cards are the lazy container; nesting them is always wrong, and it reads as depth that means nothing.                                                                    | Flatten the hierarchy — separate with spacing, type weight, and dividers.                                                                 |
| IM-009 Monotonous spacing                                | major    | One spacing value used everywhere, so nothing groups and nothing separates. Rhythm is absent, not subtle.                                                                                      | Tight steps within a group, generous steps between groups, more space above a heading than below it.                                      |
| IM-030 Content invisible at rest                         | blocker  | Text at opacity 0 or visibility hidden after every reveal handler has run — the failed-reveal signature, where content ships but never appears.                                                | Make content visible by default and let motion enhance its entrance, never gate its existence.                                            |
| IM-031 Cards flush against the scroller edge             | major    | Cards in a horizontal scroller sitting flush against one container edge while keeping a gutter on the other, so corners get clipped.                                                           | Keep a consistent inset on both sides of the scroller.                                                                                    |
| IM-032 Text occluded by an overlapping element           | blocker  | Text painted under an opaque element or a second text run, so part of it is unreadable.                                                                                                        | Give overlapping layers room, or move the text out from under the layer above it.                                                         |
| IM-033 One column stretches the first viewport           | advisory | An opening section where one column runs far past the fold while its sibling fits, leaving the short column floating in dead space.                                                            | Balance the columns, cap the tall one, or flow the long content below the opening row.                                                    |
| IM-036 Line length too long                              | major    | Text measures wider than ~80 characters, where the eye loses its place returning to the next line.                                                                                             | Constrain text containers to a 65ch–75ch measure.                                                                                         |
| IM-037 Cramped padding                                   | major    | Text landing flush against a visible boundary — a border, outline, or filled background with near-zero inset.                                                                                  | Give bordered or filled containers at least 8px of inset, and prefer 12–16px.                                                             |
| IM-038 Body text touching the viewport edge              | major    | Paragraphs rendering flush against the left or right viewport edge with no container inset.                                                                                                    | Wrap content in a container with 16px of horizontal padding at minimum, 24–32px ideally.                                                  |
| IM-041 Heading crowded against the previous block        | major    | Headings sitting as close to the block above as to their own content, so every section reads as a caption for the previous one.                                                                | Open the space above each heading so it exceeds the space below it.                                                                       |
| IM-047 Content overflowing its container                 | blocker  | Content rendering wider than its container, spilling out or forcing a page scrollbar.                                                                                                          | Let text wrap, constrain widths, or give the region a deliberate scroll affordance.                                                       |
| IM-048 Same text repeated inside one container           | advisory | The same literal text rendered three or more times in structurally different slots of one card — a label wired into every slot of a template.                                                  | Say it once, in the slot where it carries the most meaning.                                                                               |
| IM-049 Positioned child clipped by an overflow container | major    | A clipping container wrapping an absolutely positioned child, cutting off tooltips, menus, and popovers that must escape.                                                                      | Let the overflow be visible, or move the positioned layer out of the clip.                                                                |
| IM-010 Bounce or elastic easing                          | major    | Bounce and elastic easing curves. Real objects decelerate; they do not overshoot twice.                                                                                                        | Use exponential ease-out (quart, quint, expo) from an already-visible default state.                                                      |
| IM-011 Pulsing status dot                                | major    | A pulsing dot simulating liveness where nothing is actually live.                                                                                                                              | Reserve pulse for indicators bound to genuinely changing data. A static, labelled indicator is honest and calmer.                         |
| IM-012 Decorative blinking cursor                        | major    | A blinking caret animated into a hero, simulating typing where no input exists — the dev-tool aesthetic borrowed as decoration.                                                                | Let the composition hold attention. Real editable fields draw their own caret.                                                            |
| IM-017 Auto-scrolling marquee                            | major    | Continuously auto-scrolling content, which demands attention it has not earned and hides half its content at any moment.                                                                       | Show a static, legible grid and let readers move at their own pace.                                                                       |
| IM-059 Image hover transform                             | major    | Scaling or rotating an image on hover — a recurring generated-UI signature.                                                                                                                    | Let imagery sit still, or use a subtler interaction that communicates something.                                                          |
| IM-029 Layout property animation                         | major    | Animating width, height, padding, or margin, which forces layout on every frame and janks.                                                                                                     | Animate transform and opacity, or `grid-template-rows` for height reveals.                                                                |
| IM-013 Shape-assembled illustration                      | major    | A hero-scale inline SVG that builds a pictorial scene from primitive shapes — clip art, not illustration.                                                                                      | Ship real artwork, a photograph, or a deliberately drawn graphic. Icons and data graphics are fine at their own scale.                    |
| IM-028 Broken or placeholder image                       | blocker  | An image with an empty, missing, or placeholder `src`, which ships as a broken-image box.                                                                                                      | Use a real asset, generate one to brand, or remove the element and request the asset.                                                     |
| IM-023 Em-dash overuse                                   | advisory | Em-dash saturation in body copy — roughly one per 500 characters, at eight or more. Occasional use is legitimate human punctuation; the density is the tell.                                   | Prefer commas, colons, periods, or parentheses.                                                                                           |
| IM-024 Marketing buzzword                                | major    | Generic SaaS vocabulary — streamline, empower, supercharge, world-class, enterprise-grade, next-generation, cutting-edge, seamless, leverage, unlock.                                          | Name what the product literally does, with a specific verb and a specific noun.                                                           |
| IM-025 Aphoristic cadence                                | advisory | Three or more sections landing on a manufactured contrast ("Not a feature. A platform.", "X. No Y."). Once is voice; the pattern is cadence without a speaker.                                 | Write the sentence the product would actually say.                                                                                        |
| IM-057 Theater framing copy                              | advisory | Dismissing something as "theater" — a recurring generated-copy tic.                                                                                                                            | Say plainly what the thing does or does not do.                                                                                           |
| IM-058 Uncaught script error on load                     | blocker  | A script that threw or failed to parse during load. Broken JavaScript silently kills reveals and interactions, and can leave most of a page invisible.                                         | Fix the error before judging anything else on the page.                                                                                   |
